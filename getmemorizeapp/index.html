About Memorize Anything Forever

Memorize Anything Forever helps you learn and remember information using the science of spaced repetition. By reviewing items just before you're likely to forget them, you strengthen long-term memory efficiently.

Spaced Repetition is an evidence-based learning technique that incorporates increasing intervals of time between subsequent review of previously learned material to exploit the psychological spacing effect.
