Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

Introduction

Welcome to MemorizeAnything Forever ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding our mobile application MemorizeAnything Forever (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

TL:DR

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from users. We do not share any information with third parties. We do not retain any data. You have complete control over your data because it never leaves your device. If you use iCloud sync, your data is stored in iCloud and is subject to Apple's privacy policy and terms of service.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from users.

No Data Collection

MemorizeAnything Forever is designed with privacy in mind. We do not:

  • Collect any personal information
  • Store user data on our servers
  • Track user behavior or usage patterns
  • Access your memory cards or study data
  • Share any information with third parties

iCloud Sync

The App includes an optional iCloud sync feature that allows you to synchronize your data across your Apple devices. This feature is:

  • Entirely handled by Apple's iCloud service
  • Optional and can be disabled in your device settings
  • Subject to Apple's privacy policy and terms of service
  • Not accessible to us or any third parties

How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no data for us to use, process, or store.

App Functionality

The App operates entirely on your device. All your memory cards, study progress, and personal data remain on your device and are not transmitted to us or any third parties.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share any information because we do not collect any information.

No Data Sharing

Since we do not collect, store, or process any personal data, there is no information to share with third parties. Your data remains private and secure on your device.

Data Security

Your data is secure because it never leaves your device.

Local Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using iOS's secure data storage mechanisms. We do not have access to your data, and it is protected by your device's built-in security features.

Data Retention

We do not retain any data because we do not collect any data.

Your Data Control

You have complete control over your data. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete the app at any time to remove all your data, or manage your data through the app's built-in features.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data since it never leaves your device.

Data Control

Since we do not collect any personal information, traditional data rights do not apply. However, you have full control over your data because:

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • You can delete individual memory cards at any time
  • You can delete the entire app to remove all data
  • You control iCloud sync settings in your device preferences

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children, this section does not apply. The App is safe for users of all ages as no data is collected or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple's iCloud service for optional data synchronization. This service is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy and terms of service. We do not have access to any data stored in iCloud.

Memorize Pro Subscription

All subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple App Store.

Subscription Management

Memorize Pro subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not:

  • Store your payment information
  • Have access to your billing details
  • Process any financial transactions
  • Store subscription status on our servers

How to Cancel Your Subscription

To cancel your Memorize Pro subscription, you can do so in the App Store settings. Please refer to Apple's App Store terms of service for detailed information about subscription policies, refunds, and billing.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through the email address provided in the app Settings tab.
